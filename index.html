01 / PHILOSOPHY

Know your circle.
Stay inside it.

CapitalK's philosophy begins with a simple admission: we are quantitative investors at heart. We are more comfortable with data, rules and measurable signals than with subjective judgments about management teams, moats or intrinsic value.

That led us to momentum investing—where price can answer the four questions every investment process must answer: what to buy, when to buy, how much to buy and when to sell.

“The discipline isn't in choosing the right style. It's in choosing the style that matches how your mind actually works.”

02 / HOW WE INVEST

Different tools.
One disciplined framework.

Our investment and trading activities are designed around systematic, quantitative and rule-based processes. The exact rules evolve through research; the commitment to systematic decision-making does not.

01

Momentum

Cross-sectional, price-driven investing that ranks opportunities and allocates toward relative strength rather than subjective forecasts.

PRICE · RANK · ROTATE
02

Options & F&O

Systematic trading strategies designed around explicit entry, exit, position and risk rules rather than discretionary market calls.

RULES · RISK · EXECUTION
03

Research

Continuous experimentation, backtesting and refinement—with transaction costs, taxes, slippage and different market regimes treated as part of the problem.
